What the tool does

  • Uploads the owner's own original videos to the owner's own channels and sets their title, description, tags, category and scheduled publish time.
  • Manages playlists and reads comments on the owner's own videos.
  • Reads the owner's own channel and video analytics.
  • Runs read-only searches of public Shorts (titles and view counts) for content research.
